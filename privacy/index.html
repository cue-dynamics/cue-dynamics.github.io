Website Privacy Notice

Last updated: July 1, 2026

This notice covers the Site only. It does not cover the Medal platform or the gameplay clips and data processed there. If you are a Medal user and want to understand how gameplay clips, associated metadata, and derived data are collected, used, shared, or used to train AI and world models, please see the Medal Privacy Policy. Where General Intuition processes Medal data for research and model development, that processing is governed by the Medal Privacy Policy and the arrangement between General Intuition and Medal described there.

1. Who we are

General Intuition is a corporation organized under the laws of Delaware. We are a research lab building foundation models for environments that require spatial and temporal reasoning. For privacy questions about the Site, contact us at [email protected].

2. What we collect on the Site

We collect limited information when you visit the Site:

  • Usage and device data collected automatically, such as IP address, browser type, device type, pages viewed, referring page, and approximate location, gathered via analytics and our hosting provider (Webflow and any analytics tools).
  • Cookies and similar technologies used to operate the Site, remember your preferences (for example, audio or display settings), and measure traffic. See Section 6.
  • Information you choose to send us, such as your name and email address if you email us through the contact link, or information you submit if you apply for a role.

We do not sell products on the Site, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

3. How we use it

We use Site information to operate, secure, and improve the Site; to respond to your messages; to evaluate job applications; to understand how visitors use the Site; and to comply with legal obligations.

4. When we share it

We share Site information only with:

  • Service providers who host the Site and provide analytics, email, and security services, acting on our instructions;
  • Google, which provides Google Analytics. Google sets its own cookies and receives Site usage and device data (including IP address and online identifiers), and processes that data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy; 
  • Our affiliate Medal and other group/affiliated entities where reasonably necessary to operate the Site or respond to your inquiry;
  • Authorities or third parties where required by law, or to protect our rights, safety, or property; and
  • A successor entity in connection with a merger, financing, acquisition, or similar transaction.

We do not exchange personal information for money. However, the use of Google Analytics may be considered a "sale" or "share" of personal information (or "targeted advertising") under certain US state privacy laws. You can opt out of these analytics cookies as described in Sections 6 and 8.

5. Careers links

"Work with us" and "Email the team" links may take you to generalintuition.com/careers or open your email client. Recruiting data submitted through those routes is handled under the applicable Medal recruiting privacy practices.

6. Cookies

The Site is built on Webflow and uses only a limited set of cookies and similar information stored by your browser:

  • Strictly necessary / functional. Storage that is essential for the Site to work and to remember your preferences — for example, your display and audio settings (such as cell size, opacity, and mute).
  • Analytics. We use Google Analytics to understand who visits the Site and how it is used. Google Analytics sets cookies that are owned by and visible to Google, and it transmits Site usage and device data (including your IP address and online identifiers) to Google. Google processes this data under Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices. You can opt out of Google Analytics across all websites using Google's opt-out browser add-on, and you can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. You can also contact l[email protected]. Disabling some cookies may affect how the Site works.
